Offer Process

🏠 Offer submission workflow

Follow the steps below when preparing and submitting an offer.


  • ☑ Receive client instructions
  • ☑ Confirm property details
  • ☑ Review the home report
  • ☑ Review the title sheet
  • ☑ Confirm the closing date
  • ☑ Prepare the written offer
  • ☑ Submit the offer
  • ☑ Add the file note
  • ☑ Schedule a follow-up reminder
  • ☑ Update the Support 1 channel
  • ☑ Obtain the delivery receipt from Khushi or Shrishti

Assignment Rules

⚖️ Rules


  • Cases should be assigned to the conveyancing team only during the offer stage.
  • At every other stage, the matter should remain with the New Business team.

Accepted Offer Process

✅ Accepted offer workflow


  • ☑ Notify the client.
  • ☑ Update the matter.
  • ☑ Obtain identification documents.
  • ☑ Complete AML checks.
  • ☑ Issue documentation.
  • ☑ Contact the estate agent.
